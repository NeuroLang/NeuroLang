Add a user, or change one

A user named by an ORCID iD (0000-0002-1825-0097) signs in with ORCID, and needs no password. Merged into a user of a name of their own (merge into… in the list above), that user signs in with the iD too; Separate ORCID makes it a user of its own again.

A user's assistant: the key their page gives (if allowed), else their own (set here or on their account page), else their groups', else the server's, else none: they give theirs in the page.
